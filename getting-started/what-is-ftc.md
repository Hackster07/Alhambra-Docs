# What is FTC?

FTC (First Tech Challenge) is a 6-12 grade level robotics competition that encourages students to learn many principles from engineering, coding, teamwork and even business, all packed into one program. Each season, they introduce a new theme and challenge for students to solve with high levels of creativity and efficiency in order to compete against other students around the world.
