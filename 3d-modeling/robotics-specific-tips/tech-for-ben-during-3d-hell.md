### Joining parts together

{% hint style="info" %}
When creating a new design in Fusion, make sure you use the Hybrid design option, as it grants you access to all the tools without getting confused where they are.
{% endhint %}

The tool I would use is called **Joint** in the Assemblies tab, where you click on the desired destination edge, then the edge of the part that you want to move.
